2. STANDARD DEDUCTIONS (2026)
- Married Filing Jointly: $32,200
- Single / Married Filing Separately: $16,100
- Head of Household: $24,150
- OBBBA Senior Deduction (Age 65+): $12,000 (MFJ) / $6,000 (Single)
  *Full deduction below $150,000 MAGI (MFJ) / $75,000 (Single). 
  *Phases out completely at $250,000 MAGI (MFJ) / $175,000 (Single).
  *Available whether taking the standard deduction or itemizing.

3. DEDUCTIONS & MILEAGE
- SALT Cap (State and Local Taxes): $40,400 (MFJ / Single) / $20,200 (MFS)
  *Phase-down begins at $505,000 MAGI (MFJ) / $252,500 (MFS); floor is $10,000.
- IRS Business Mileage Rate: 72.5 cents per mile
- IRS Medical/Moving Mileage: 20.5 cents per mile
- Charitable Deduction (Non-Itemizers): Up to $2,000 (MFJ) / $1,000 (Single) above-the-line for cash gifts.
- Charitable Deduction (Itemizers): Subject to a 0.5% AGI floor for itemized donations.

4. FAMILY & EDUCATION CREDITS
- Child Tax Credit (CTC): $2,200 per child (Partially refundable up to $1,700)
- Dependent Care Credit: Up to $6,000 for 2+ children (Max 35% of expenses)
- 529 Contribution (Gift Tax Exclusion): $19,000 (Individual) / $38,000 (MFJ)
- 529 Superfunding (5-Year Election): $95,000 (Individual) / $190,000 (MFJ)

5. RETIREMENT & SAVINGS LIMITS (2026)
- 401(k), 403(b), 457(b) Employee Contribution Limit: $24,500
  *Standard Catch-Up (Age 50–59 & 64+): +$8,000 (Total $32,500)
  *SECURE 2.0 Super Catch-Up (Ages 60–63): +$11,250 (Total $35,750)
- IRA Contribution Limit: $7,500 
  *Catch-Up (Age 50+): +$1,100 (Total $8,600)
- HSA Contribution Limit: $8,750 (Family) / $4,400 (Self-Only)
  *Catch-Up (Age 55+): +$1,000

6. SURCHARGES & TAX THRESHOLDS
- Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT): 3.8% on MAGI over $250,000 (MFJ) / $200,000 (Single)
- Additional Medicare Tax: 0.9% on wages over $250,000 (MFJ) / $200,000 (Single)

7. CALIFORNIA STATE TAXES (2026 ESTIMATED)
- CA Standard Deduction: $11,720 (MFJ) / $5,860 (Single)
- CA State Disability Insurance (SDI): 1.3% (No wage ceiling)
- CA Dependent Credit: $488 per dependent
- CA Personal Exemption Credit: $314 (MFJ) / $157 (Single)
- CA Young Child Tax Credit: $1,117 (Qualifying child under age 6)
- CA 529 Tax Deduction: NO STATE DEDUCTION
  *(SB 529 / AB 2444 did not pass; California does not allow a state deduction for 529 contributions).
